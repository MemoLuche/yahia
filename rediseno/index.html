About Dr. Yahia

Elhadi M. Yahia, Ph.D.

Emeritus Professor & Senior Scientist at the Universidad Autónoma de Querétaro (UAQ), Mexico. Dr. Yahia holds a B.Sc. from the University of Tripoli, an M.Sc. from UC Davis, and a Ph.D. from Cornell University.

His research group focuses on the identification, characterization and quantification of phytochemicals in fruits and vegetables, their bioavailability and bioaccessibility, and their biological functions and nutritional importance. He collaborates with research groups in Mexico and across the globe.

What We Study

Research Focus Areas

Multidisciplinary research connecting plant science, food technology, and human nutrition.

Phytochemicals

Identification, characterization, and quantification of bioactive compounds — polyphenols, carotenoids, vitamins — in fruits and vegetables.

Bioavailability & Bioaccessibility

Understanding how phytochemicals are released from food matrices and absorbed by the human body to exert health effects.

Postharvest Physiology

Studying the biochemical and physiological changes in fruits and vegetables after harvest to extend shelf life and preserve quality.

Nutrition & Health

Evaluating the nutritional composition and health-promoting properties of fresh and processed foods for human well-being.

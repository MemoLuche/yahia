Dr. Elhadi M. Yahia

Professor & Researcher — Postharvest Biology & Technology

Facultad de Ciencias Naturales, Universidad Autónoma de Querétaro, México

Dr. Elhadi Yahia is one of the world's leading experts in postharvest biology and technology of fresh fruits and vegetables. Born in Algeria, he has built a career dedicated to one fundamental question: how do we keep food fresh, nutritious, and safe from the farm to the consumer?

Over more than four decades, Dr. Yahia has authored or co-authored over 700 publications, including more than 25 books that are used as reference texts in universities across five continents. He has collaborated with farmers, governments, research institutions, and international organizations such as the FAO in more than 40 countries.

His work has had a direct impact on reducing food loss and waste, improving nutrition outcomes, and helping smallholder farmers in Latin America, Africa, and Asia bring their produce to market in better condition. He is the founder and leader of the Phytochemicals and Nutrition Laboratory at UAQ, where his team conducts cutting-edge research on food quality, antioxidants, and tropical fruits.

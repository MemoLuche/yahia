Summer of research

Agriculture, Ninetheme, Organic, Vegatables
20 Jun
Elhadi yahia

Students from the Autonomous University of Chiapas and University of Sciences and Arts of Chiapas, Maybelline Reyes, Raquel Reynosa and Mariana Montes are currently working on the Phytochemicals and Nutrition Laboratory, at the Autonomous University of Querétaro, as part of a summer research program during june and july of 2022. These students, from the area of nutrition and biotechnology, are the first accepted students in the laboratory, after de pandemic situation.
